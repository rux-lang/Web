# Community

Rux is built in the open, and everyone is welcome — whether you want to follow along, ask a question, or share what you are building.

## Stay Updated

Follow Rux on your favorite network to get early updates, dev logs, and sneak peeks.

## Join the Conversation

Ask questions, share what you are building, and talk to the team and other Rux developers.

## Get Involved

Want to contribute code or support the project financially? Head over to the [Support](/support) page.
